Browse CAFANS product listings ↗
01

Choose a category first

Begin with the item you actually need: sneakers, a hoodie, a jersey or an accessory. Keep the shortlist focused on comparable products.

02

Read the individual listing

Check the seller, material, color, measurements and included items. A spreadsheet summary may omit details or become outdated.

03

Review fit and available evidence

Use measurement charts and clear product images. If inspection photos are provided, examine visible construction and the ordered variant; images alone do not prove authenticity.

04

Estimate the complete cost

Record the item price alongside shipping, service charges and any applicable import costs. Confirm current purchasing and return conditions before paying.

Clear answers

Before you browse.

Short answers to common questions about discovery lists, product details and this independent guide.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list associated with shopping through Kakobuy. Lists may organize product links, categories, prices and notes. Their accuracy varies, so verify each listing before purchasing.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial discovery page that links to CAFANS. It is not operated by or endorsed by Kakobuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.

Does a spreadsheet prove a branded item is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet entry, image or brand name does not establish authenticity. Check seller identity, provenance, product details and the applicable return policy.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check units and the seller’s measurement method rather than relying only on an S, M or L label.

What belongs in a total cost estimate?

Include the item price, any domestic shipping, service charges, international shipping and applicable taxes or duties. Use current provider quotes because rates and conditions vary.

Where do the category links go?

The ten category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. Other navigation and shopping links open the CAFANS homepage.
